Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

1. Overview

Smart Format Pro is a Google Docs productivity add-on built to assist business analysts, technical writers, and teams with table formatting, heading theme styling, and requirements structure.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Smart Format Pro accesses your active document exclusively to read and apply styling (such as text margins, table widths, and heading hierarchies) when initiated by you.

3. Data Storage & Privacy

Smart Format Pro does not store, collect, or transmit any document content to external servers. All operations are processed locally and in-session.

4. Google API Limited Use Policy

Smart Format Pro's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
